## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: Traditional German *Bewerbungsmappe* (cover letter + tabular CV + optional cover sheet) created in a guided German web form with XING profile import.

**Target audience**: Traditional employees, clerks, and mainstream applicants in Germany who want a classic German *Bewerbung* without designing it in Word.

**Positioning angle**: Traditional DACH application portal with pre-written German text snippets (*Textbausteine*) and XING integration.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- **High Organic Visibility in Germany**: Exact-match domain (`lebenslauf.de`) ranks at the top of German Google search results for *"Lebenslauf erstellen"*.
- **Familiar German Formalities**: Understands German date formats, photo conventions, signature lines, and XING integration.

### Weaknesses
- **Watermark Paywall**: Users invest 30–45 minutes filling out their entire work history only to discover that downloading a usable, watermark-free PDF requires paying €16.99+.
- **Not Built for Software Engineers or International Tech**: Templates look like traditional administrative CVs, lack bilingual EN/DE toggle support, and cap users at 5 profiles.
- **Clichéd *Textbausteine* (Canned Phrases)**: Pre-written German snippets produce generic, outdated 2010-era phrasing rather than quantified STAR engineering achievements.
- **Multi-Column / Graphic Templates Hurt ATS Parsing**: Several sidebar-heavy designs struggle with modern international ATS platforms (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever) used by German tech scaleups.
