## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: Bookmark jobs from LinkedIn/Indeed via a Chrome extension, score your resume against the job description's keywords, and use built-in AI credits to rewrite bullets.

**Target audience**: Active US job seekers looking for an all-in-one browser workspace to track applications and optimize keyword match scores.

**Positioning angle**: AI-powered career CRM and keyword matching engine.

---

## Product & Features

### Core Capabilities
- **Chrome Extension Job Clipper**: Bookmarks job postings from major boards into a cloud CRM table.
- **Keyword Match Scoring**: Highlights hard and soft skills in the job description and calculates a match percentage (full keyword list locked behind Teal+).
- **AI Bullet & Cover Letter Writer**: Built-in LLM wrapper for generating resume summaries, achievement bullets, and basic cover letters.
- **Multi-Version Resume Manager**: Toggles checkboxes on work experience bullets to assemble role-specific resumes.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths
- **Strong SEO & Content Marketing Funnel**: Ranks heavily for resume examples, career change guides, and job tracker keywords.
- **Convenient Browser Clipper**: Chrome extension lowers friction for saving job postings while browsing.

### Weaknesses
- **Expensive Subscription ($13/week / $29/month) for Thin LLM Wrappers**: Teal charges up to $52/month primarily to unlock prompt templates and keyword extraction that candidates can run with much higher quality using their own frontier LLMs (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) and structured prompts.
- **US-Centric & Non-Compliant with German Standards**: Built for Anglo-Saxon resumes; lacks DIN 5008 cover letter formatting, German notice period/salary conventions, bilingual EN/DE synchronization, and European CV photo/layout expectations.
- **Generic "AI Slop" Output**: Teal's built-in AI generator frequently produces repetitive, buzzword-heavy phrasing that experienced technical recruiters immediately recognize and reject.
- **Closed Ecosystem**: Cannot be version-controlled in Git, customized at the HTML/CSS level, or self-hosted as a live personal portfolio domain.

## Competitive Implications for Career Engine

**Where they're strong vs. us**:
- Browser extension convenience for non-technical US job seekers who want a hosted GUI tracker.

**Where we're strong vs. them**:
- **Transparent, Frontier-Grade Prompt Library & 24 Agent Skills for Free**: Instead of charging $29/month for a black-box LLM wrapper, Career Engine provides the exact battle-tested system prompts (`jobs/prompts/`) and 24 specialized agent skills (`skills/*`) that work with Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, or local models at zero subscription cost.
- **Engineered for the German & European Market**: Full DIN 5008 compliance, bilingual EN/DE switchability, spaced compound technical term rules, and regional search platforms (StepStone, XING, Arbeitsagentur, Workwise, Yourfirm).
